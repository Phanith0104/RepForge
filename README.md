# RepForge 🏋️‍♂️🤸‍♀️

**RepForge** is the ultimate, all-in-one fitness companion built natively in **Kotlin** for Android. Designed for fitness enthusiasts of all ages and background levels, RepForge seamlessly bridges the gap between traditional **Gym Weight Training**, **Calisthenics Progressions**, and **Personalized Body Transformation (Weight Gain & Fat Loss)**.

Whether you are stepping into a gym for the first time, attempting your first pull-up, or mastering advanced bodyweight skills, RepForge provides the tools, structure, and tracking you need to forge your ultimate physique.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏋️ 1. Comprehensive Gym Training & Specific Muscle Targeting
Dedicated tracking, exercise libraries, and structured routines targeting every muscle group with precision:
- **Chest:** Bench press variations, cable flyes, push-up progressions, and upper/lower chest isolation.
- **Back:** Lat pulldowns, heavy rows, deadlifts, and back thickness routines.
- **Shoulders:** Overhead presses, lateral raises, front raises, and rear delt conditioning.
- **Forearms & Grip:** Hammer curls, wrist curls, plate pinches, and dedicated grip-strength builders.
- **Legs & Arms:** Complete leg day setups and arm armories.

### 🤸 2. Calisthenics Mastery (Zero to Advanced)
RepForge meets you exactly where you are on your bodyweight journey:
- **Complete Beginner Track:** Fundamental movements (wall push-ups, knee push-ups, dead hangs, Australian pull-ups) to build initial tendon strength and joint endurance.
- **Intermediate to Advanced Progressions:** Step-by-step skill trees for mastering:
  - Muscle-ups
  - Handstand push-ups (HSPU)
  - Front Lever & Back Lever
  - Planche progressions
  - Pistol squats & Single-arm pull-ups

### ⚖️ 3. All-in-One Body Transformation (Weight Gain & Fat Loss)
- **Goal-Oriented Workouts:** Specialized hypertrophy programs for muscle mass gain and high-intensity hybrid workouts for aggressive calorie burn and fat loss.
- **Smart Calorie & Macro Planning:** Calorie, TDEE, and macronutrient calculators built right into the app to optimize your body composition.
- **Progress Tracking:** Log your body weight, body fat percentage, and measurements over time with dynamic visuals.

### 📱 4. Built for Everyone
- **Universal Accessibility:** Suitable for all age groups, from young beginners to mature lifters looking for structured strength maintenance.
- **Native Android Performance:** Smooth, ultra-responsive native Android application built 100% in Kotlin.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **Language:** [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
- **UI Framework:** Jetpack Compose / XML Layouts
- **Architecture:** Clean Architecture with MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
- **Database:** Room Database for persistent local data logging
- **Asynchronous Processing:** Kotlin Coroutines & Flow
- **Dependency Injection:** Hilt / Dagger
